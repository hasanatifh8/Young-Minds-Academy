The Problem

Why Traditional Tests Fail Students & Parents

When a Class 9 or 10 student scores 45%, the usual advice is “study harder, solve more sample papers, take more tuition.” Rote practice doesn't fix invisible gaps.

Raw scores hide the cause

A mark of 14/25 shows that marks were lost — but says nothing about why.

The hidden bottleneck

Struggles with advanced topics like quadratic equations or kinematics very often come from unresolved Class 6–8 basics — negative-sign transposition, fraction reciprocals or ratios.

The anxiety loop

Students decide they are “bad at maths or science,” leading to exam anxiety, frustration and falling confidence.

The Solution

Meet the Foundation Gap Radar™

It breaks every topic into small concepts and traces each error back to where it started.

How It Works

The 5-Step Diagnostic Cycle

Step 1

15-Minute Adaptive Test

Questions adjust to the student's answers. When an error appears, the test steps back to check the prerequisite concept, without overwhelming the student.

Step 2

Gap & Root-Cause Scan

Each topic is broken into small concepts and traced back up to 2–3 grades to find conceptual gaps and misconceptions.

Step 3

Clear Reports for Everyone

Parents get clarity, students get a confidence debrief, and mentors get a prescription of exactly what to fix first.

Step 4

Targeted Micro-Practice

1–2 page worksheets on the identified gaps only, followed by a 5-minute re-test to measure improvement.

Step 5

Ongoing Mastery Tracking

The student's profile keeps updating, with spaced-revision reminders so fixed concepts stay fixed.

Root-Cause Scan

Four Reasons Marks Are Lost

The report shows not just which answers were wrong, but the kind of mistake behind each one.

Conceptual Gaps

A core principle was never fully understood and needs to be re-taught.

Procedural Slips

The concept is clear, but steps are executed with mechanical mistakes.

Reading Traps

Constraints in word problems are misread or misinterpreted.

Pacing Pressure

Errors cluster when the student is working against the clock.

FAQ

Questions Parents Ask

Is this another high-pressure entrance exam?
No. The Foundation Gap Radar™ is a diagnostic tool, not a pass/fail test. It takes 15–20 minutes, adapts to the student, and is designed to build confidence by showing strengths alongside gaps that can be fixed.
Which classes and subjects does it cover?
Students from Class 6 to Class 12, with a focus on foundational Mathematics and Science (Physics and Chemistry).
How soon do we get the results?
Right after the scan. The report is shared with parents on WhatsApp and email, and our mentor walks you through it.
Does it cost anything?
No. The 15-minute scan and its report are complimentary.
What happens after the scan?
You get a short, targeted practice plan for the gaps that were found. A quick re-test afterwards shows how much has improved.
