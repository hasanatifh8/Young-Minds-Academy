Course Overview

Score Higher in Your Board Exams

Class 10 boards are a student's first big public exam. Our program covers Maths, Science, English and Social Science chapter by chapter, with special focus on NCERT, sample papers and the answer-writing techniques examiners look for.

Complete board syllabus finished well before exams
NCERT exercises and exemplar problems solved in class
Chapter-wise previous-year questions
Answer-writing and presentation practice

The Course Includes

  • Detailed notes, formula sheets and mind maps
  • Weekly chapter tests and monthly cumulative tests
  • Three full-length mock board exams
  • Revision crash course before pre-boards
  • Personal doubt sessions every week

FAQ

Course Questions

Answers to common questions about this program.

Which subjects are included?
Mathematics, Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), English and Social Science. You can also join for individual subjects.
Do you prepare students for Olympiads and foundation exams too?
Yes. An optional JEE/NEET Foundation module can be added for students planning to take up Science after Class 10.
When will the syllabus be completed?
We aim to complete the syllabus by December so that January and February are dedicated to revision and mock exams.
Are there separate batches for Class 9 and 10?
Yes. Class 9 and Class 10 have separate batches, further divided by board.
