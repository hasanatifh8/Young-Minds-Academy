FAQ

Admission Questions

Common questions about joining, fees and scholarships.

When do new batches start?
New batches for most courses start in April and June. Mid-session admissions are accepted throughout the year with catch-up classes.
Is the scholarship test compulsory?
No. It's optional, but it's free and helps us place students in the right batch — and may earn a fee waiver.
Can fees be paid in instalments?
Yes. Fees can be paid in up to three instalments. Details are shared during counselling.
What is your refund policy?
Please see our Terms & Conditions page for the complete refund and withdrawal policy.
Can my child switch between online and classroom mode?
Yes, students can switch modes subject to seat availability in the chosen batch.
