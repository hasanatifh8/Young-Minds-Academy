Beyond the Classroom

Smart Learning Support for Every Student

Tools that keep students on track between classes and keep parents informed about their progress.

Free Diagnostic Test

A 15-minute test on the first day of the demo finds your child's exact concept gaps, and you get a short report on WhatsApp.

3-Day Progress Card

After the trial classes, see a before-and-after comparison of how much your child improved in just three days.

Concept-Wise Progress Reports

Monthly reports show mastery topic by topic, like "Algebra: 85% mastered", instead of just raw marks.

Early Support Alerts

If attendance or test scores start slipping, a mentor steps in with one-on-one help before your child falls behind.

Daily Parent Prompts

Each evening you get one question on WhatsApp to ask your child about what they learned in class that day.

Homework Snap & Feedback

Students photograph their homework. It's checked step by step, verified by a teacher, and corrections come back on WhatsApp.

Personalised Revision Kits

Before exams, each student gets a 2-page practice sheet focused only on the topics they found hardest.

Missed-Class Catch-Up

Absent? Students receive a summary, key formulas and practice questions, plus quick daily revision flashcards.

For Parents

Children rarely answer that question with more than "nothing". Our evening WhatsApp prompt gives you one interesting question about that day's lesson, so your child gets to explain what they learned.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents and students usually ask before joining.

How do I book a free demo class?
Fill in the demo class form on this page, call us on +91 91296 04415 or message us on WhatsApp. Our counsellor will call you within 24 hours to fix a convenient day and time.
How many students are there in a batch?
We keep batches small — a maximum of 20 students — so that every student gets personal attention and can ask questions freely.
Which boards do you cover?
We teach students from CBSE, ICSE and State Board schools. Batches are organised by class and board so the syllabus and exam pattern match exactly.
Do you offer online classes?
Yes. Every course is available in classroom, live online and hybrid modes. Online students get the same study material, tests and doubt support.
What are the fees? Are scholarships available?
Fees depend on the course and class. Scholarships of up to 50% are available through our Scholarship Test, held twice a month. See the Admissions page for details.
How will I know how my child is doing?
Students take weekly chapter tests. Parents receive a detailed progress report every month and are invited to regular parent–teacher meetings.
