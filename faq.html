Help Centre

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our courses, batches, admissions, fees, tests and online classes.

General

What makes Young Minds Academy different from other coaching centres?
We focus on concept-first teaching in small batches of no more than 20 students. Every student takes weekly tests, receives a monthly progress report, and has access to our daily doubt desk.
Where is the academy located?
Our centre is at 10A/8, Tashkand Marg, near Patrika Chauraha, Opp. Saket Colony, Prayagraj – 211001. We also offer live online classes for students who can't attend in person.
What are your timings?
Classes run Monday to Saturday from 7 AM to 8:30 PM depending on the batch. Sundays are reserved for tests and doubt-clearing sessions.
Which boards do you teach?
We teach CBSE, ICSE and State Board students, with separate batches so that the syllabus and exam pattern match exactly.

Courses & Batches

Which courses do you offer?
Pre-Nurture (Class 5–8), Pre-Foundation (Class 9–10), Foundation 11–12 in PCB, PCMB and Commerce, CUET batches, and Target & Foundation for CLAT, IPMAT and CAT.
How many students are in a batch?
We keep batches small so every student gets personal attention. Ask our counsellor about current batch sizes.
Can my child join for just one subject?
Yes. Students can enrol for individual subjects in most courses. Fees are adjusted accordingly.
What are Regular and Platinum batches?
Most courses are offered in a Regular batch and a Platinum batch. Our counsellor will explain the timings and extra support in each so you can choose.

Admissions & Fees

How do I get admission?
Book a free demo class, take the scholarship/diagnostic test, meet our counsellor and complete enrolment. See the Admissions page for details.
Are scholarships available?
Yes. Our free Scholarship Test, held on the 2nd and 4th Sunday of every month, offers fee waivers of up to 50%.
Can fees be paid in instalments?
Yes, fees can be paid in up to three instalments. Details are shared during counselling.
Is there a sibling discount?
Yes, we offer a discount for siblings enrolled at the academy. Please ask our front desk for current offers.

Tests & Progress

How often are tests conducted?
Weekly chapter tests for all courses, plus monthly cumulative tests and full-length mock exams before boards and entrance exams.
How will parents know about their child's progress?
Parents receive a detailed progress report every month and are invited to parent–teacher meetings. You can also call your child's mentor anytime.
What if my child misses a class?
Students can attend the same topic in another batch or watch a recorded session, and can clear any doubts at the daily doubt desk.

Online Classes

Are online classes live or recorded?
All online classes are live and interactive. Recordings are shared afterwards for revision.
Do online students get study material and tests?
Yes. Online students receive the same printed material by courier and take the same tests online.
What do I need for online classes?
A laptop, tablet or smartphone with a stable internet connection and a quiet place to study.
